# Review of the reliability revision

## Scientific assessment

The revision resolves a narrow, reproducible valid-repair failure at the evidence-selection boundary. It preserves factual verification and invalid controls. It also implements alternative release policies that remove five known false target releases in a fixed-state replay, without losing the other target-passing releases in that archive. These are useful diagnostic contributions.

The strongest limitation is generalization. Development agreement improves under soft arbitration, but the separate ANLI R2 evaluation has no aggregate gain and one additional false contradiction. The soft configuration is not promoted. The archived combined candidate remains worse on fixed-card correct releases, and those adverse results remain in the paper. The new component replay does not supersede that whole-graph result.

External comparisons are still insufficient for a strong comparative factuality claim. The common adapters and their resource controls are implemented and tested, but the live pilot stopped after three provider failures. Only 12 usable records exist from 80 planned combinations. The published-method adapters are approximations, and the frozen evidence pool is derived from the earlier graph. No ranking is justified. An updated full-graph prospective comparison on matched natural errors is not completed.

Independent human annotation is pending. The two CSVs contain 309 items but no completed human labels. Target relation checks, known fictional cards, software assertions and public ANLI reference labels have distinct validity. Kappa tooling and adjudication guidelines prepare an evaluation; they do not make the labels independent or complete.

The Detector is appropriately described as advisory. Its removal affects no Judge action in the observed states and no operational saving is demonstrated. Memory provenance now survives storage, but reads do not participate in answer generation and cross-store atomicity remains absent. Neither component supports a factuality gain claim.

Originality remains an architectural and diagnostic research argument, not proof that no earlier system combines similar ideas. The related-work discussion preserves verified technical precedents and the distinction between preprints and peer-reviewed papers. Patent novelty and eligibility have not been established by these experiments.

## Editorial assessment

The manuscript retains its original section order and detailed seven-agent methods, with added inputs, policy conditions, examples and limits where behavior changed. Tables state meaningful events, units and denominators. Numeric references retain first-citation ordering. The conclusion contains two paragraphs immediately followed by References. The supplement separates preserved historical measurements from the new series.

Four scientific figure corrections were explicitly approved. The user subsequently approved landscape arrangements for Figures 3, 5 and 7. These are placed in wide floats with the original left or right alignment, restoring the earlier layout. All 25 final article pages, 14 supplement pages and five unchanged disclosure pages were visually inspected. The original image files remain in the editable source.

The manuscript is suitable for substantive author and faculty review after layout validation. It should not be represented as submission-ready scientific evidence of general hallucination reduction. Remaining work includes independent annotation, a completed strong baseline comparison, representative natural errors, prospective matched full-graph evaluation, and better evidence/identity generalization.
